• Universities rely on fragmented systems causing data silos.
• Students juggle multiple, disconnected platforms.
• Lack of integration leads to administrative inefficiency.
The UniSphere Fix
• UniSphere: A web-based, A1-powered unified platform.
• Centralizes all essential student services.
